## Getting Started

### System Requirements

- iOS 13.0 or later
- Android 8.0 (API level 26) or later
- Stable internet connection (Wi-Fi or cellular data)
- Location services enabled (for nearby events)
- Camera access (for QR code scanning and image uploads)

### Installation

1. **iOS Users**:
   - Open the App Store
   - Search for "ScanGo"
   - Tap "Get" or the download icon
   - Follow the on-screen instructions to complete installation

2. **Android Users**:
   - Open the Google Play Store
   - Search for "ScanGo"
   - Tap "Install"
   - Follow the on-screen instructions to complete installation

### App Permissions

When you first launch ScanGo, you'll be asked to grant the following permissions:

- **Location**: To find events near you
- **Camera**: For QR code scanning and taking photos
- **Photo Library**: To upload images from your device
- **Notifications**: To receive updates about events and social interactions

You can adjust these permissions later in your device settings.

## User Registration and Login

### Creating a New Account

1. Launch the ScanGo app
2. Tap "Sign Up" on the welcome screen
3. Enter your email address and create a password
4. Tap "Continue"
5. Complete your profile information:
   - Full name
   - Profile picture (optional)
   - Phone number (optional)
   - Location (optional)
6. Review and accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
7. Tap "Create Account"

### Signing In with Google

1. Launch the ScanGo app
2. Tap "Continue with Google" on the welcome screen
3. Select your Google account
4. Review the permissions and tap "Continue"
5. Complete any additional profile information if prompted
6. Review and accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
7. Tap "Complete Sign Up"

### Logging In to an Existing Account

1. Launch the ScanGo app
2. Enter your email and password
3. Tap "Log In"

### Resetting Your Password

1. Launch the ScanGo app
2. Tap "Forgot Password?" on the login screen
3. Enter your email address
4. Tap "Send Reset Link"
5. Check your email for the password reset link
6. Follow the instructions in the email to reset your password

## Navigating the App

### Home Screen

The Home screen is your central hub for discovering events and accessing key features:

- **Featured Event**: Highlighted at the top of the screen
- **Upcoming Events**: Events happening soon
- **Events Near You**: Events in your vicinity
- **Your Events**: Events you've created or are attending
- **Category Buttons**: Quick access to events by category
- **Create Event Button**: Floating "+" button to create a new event
- **Search Bar**: "Explore all events" button to search for events

### Main Navigation

The bottom navigation bar provides access to the main sections of the app:

- **Home**: Discover events and see your feed
- **Explore**: Search and filter events
- **Create**: Create a new event
- **Notifications**: View your notifications
- **Profile**: Access your profile and settings

## Discovering Events

### Browsing Events

1. On the Home screen, scroll through the different event sections:
   - Featured Event
   - Upcoming Events
   - Events Near You

2. Tap on any event card to view its details

### Searching for Events

1. Tap the "Explore all events" button on the Home screen
2. Enter keywords in the search bar
3. Use filters to refine your search:
   - Date range
   - Location radius
   - Category
   - Price (free or paid)
   - Privacy level

### Using Categories

1. On the Home screen, scroll horizontally through the category buttons
2. Tap a category to see events in that category
3. Browse the filtered events
4. Tap on any event to view its details

### Finding Nearby Events

1. Tap the map icon in the header of the Home screen
2. View events plotted on the map
3. Tap on a map marker to see a preview of the event
4. Tap on the preview to view the full event details

## Creating and Managing Events

### Creating a New Event

1. Tap the "+" floating button on the Home screen or the "Create" tab in the navigation bar
2. Fill in the event details:
   - Event title
   - Description
   - Date and time
   - Location
   - Category
   - Cover image
   - Privacy level (public, connections-only, private)
   - Ticket information (free or paid)
3. Tap "Preview" to see how your event will look
4. Tap "Create Event" to publish your event

### Editing an Event

1. Go to your Profile tab
2. Tap "Your Events"
3. Select the event you want to edit
4. Tap the "Edit" button
5. Make your changes
6. Tap "Save Changes"

### Managing Attendees

1. Go to your Profile tab
2. Tap "Your Events"
3. Select the event you want to manage
4. Tap "Attendees"
5. View the list of attendees
6. Use the options to:
   - Send a message to attendees
   - Export attendee list
   - Remove attendees (if necessary)

### Canceling an Event

1. Go to your Profile tab
2. Tap "Your Events"
3. Select the event you want to cancel
4. Tap the "More" menu (three dots)
5. Select "Cancel Event"
6. Provide a reason for cancellation (optional)
7. Tap "Confirm Cancellation"

## Using the Social Feed

### Finding and Following Users

1. Go to the Explore tab

3. Browse suggested users or search for specific users

## QR Code Scanning

### Scanning an Event QR Code

1. Tap the QR code icon in the header of the Home screen
2. Point your camera at the event QR code
3. Hold steady until the code is recognized
4. View the event details that appear
5. Tap "Check In" to confirm your attendance

### Generating a QR Code for Your Event

1. Go to your Profile tab
2. Tap "Your Events"
3. Select the event you want to generate a QR code for
4. Tap "QR Code"
5. The QR code will be displayed on screen
6. Tap "Share" to send the QR code to others
7. Tap "Save" to save the QR code to your device

## Troubleshooting

### App Crashes or Freezes

1. Force close the app:
   - iOS: Swipe up from the bottom of the screen (or double-tap the home button on older devices) and swipe the app up
   - Android: Tap the square/recent apps button and swipe the app away
2. Restart your device
3. Reopen the app
4. If the issue persists, check for app updates in the App Store or Google Play Store

### Login Issues

1. Verify your internet connection
2. Double-check your email and password
3. Try the "Forgot Password" option to reset your password
4. If using Google Sign-In, ensure you're connected to the internet and try again
5. If problems persist, contact support through the app

### Location Services Not Working

1. Check if location services are enabled for the app:
   - iOS: Settings > Privacy > Location Services > ScanGo
   - Android: Settings > Apps > ScanGo > Permissions > Location
2. Ensure your device's location services are turned on
3. Try restarting the app
4. If the issue persists, restart your device

### Image Upload Failures

1. Check your internet connection
2. Verify the image isn't too large (recommended: under 10MB)
3. Ensure you've granted the app permission to access your photos
4. Try selecting a different image
5. If uploading from camera, try taking a new photo

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is ScanGo free to use?**  
A: Yes, ScanGo is free to download and use. Some events may have ticket costs, but the app itself is free.

**Q: Can I use ScanGo offline?**  
A: Some features of ScanGo work offline, but you'll need an internet connection to refresh content, create events, and interact with other users.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**  
A: Go to Profile > Settings > Account > Delete Account. Follow the prompts to confirm deletion.

### Events

**Q: How many events can I create?**  
A: There is no limit to the number of events you can create.

**Q: Can I make my event private?**  
A: Yes, when creating or editing an event, you can set the privacy level to "Private" so only invited users can see it.

**Q: How do I cancel my attendance to an event?**  
A: Go to the event details page and tap "Cancel Attendance" or "Not Going."

### Social Features

**Q: Who can see my posts?**  
A: This depends on your privacy settings. You can set posts to be visible to everyone, only your connections, or just yourself.

**Q: How do I remove a comment on my post?**  
A: Tap and hold on the comment, then select "Delete" from the options that appear.

**Q: Can I block another user?**  
A: Yes, go to their profile, tap the three dots in the top right, and select "Block User."

### Technical Support

**Q: How do I report a bug?**  
A: Go to Profile > Settings > Help & Support > Report a Problem.

**Q: How do I contact customer support?**  
A: Go to Profile > Settings > Help & Support > Contact Us.

**Q: How often is the app updated?**  
A: We regularly release updates with new features and bug fixes. Make sure to keep your app updated to the latest version.
